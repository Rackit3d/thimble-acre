ABOUT
Thimble Acre is a small farm that keeps growing while you're away. Plant turnips and pumpkins, pump the well, and pick what's ripe. Hire farmhands and they'll water, plant and pick for you.

Pair two animals and see what hatches. Coats pass down from both parents, and some are rare. Two rare parents might give you an epic, and two epics a legendary. The Coat Book keeps every coat you've found: 189 of them, across eight kinds of animal.

FEATURES
- 189 coats across hens, ducks, rabbits, cows, sheep, pigs, goats and donkeys
- 32 crops, with mutations to find in the Seed Almanac
- Village orders, seasons and summer fetes with prizes
- Answer your animals' wants, pull weeds, shoo the crow, chase off the fox
- Send a postcard of a new hatch, or let a friend's animal stay a day on your farm
