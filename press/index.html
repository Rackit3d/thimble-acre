About the game

Thimble Acre is a small farm that keeps growing while you're away. Plant turnips and pumpkins, pump the well, and pick what's ripe. Hire farmhands and they'll water, plant and pick for you, so there's always something waiting when you come back.

Every baby is a surprise. Pair two animals and see what hatches. Coats pass down from both parents, and some are rare. Two rare parents might give you an epic, and two epics a legendary. The Coat Book keeps every coat you've found: 189 of them, across eight kinds of animal.

Features

  • 189 coats to collect across hens, ducks, rabbits, cows, sheep, pigs, goats and donkeys
  • 32 crops, with mutations to find in the Seed Almanac
  • Village orders, seasons and summer fetes with prizes
  • Hands-on play: answer your animals' wants, pull weeds, shoo the crow, chase off the fox
  • Mabel next door shows you how each new thing works
  • Send a postcard of a new hatch, or give a friend four words so one of your animals can stay a day on their farm
  • Pop in for a minute or stay for an hour
