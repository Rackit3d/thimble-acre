Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Thimble Acre is made to be played without handing over anything about you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

What stays on your iPhone

Your farm, its settings and everything you name in the game are saved on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, except what you choose to send with a visitor code (below).

Visitor codes

If you choose to send an animal to visit a friend, the game makes a four-word code and stores a small record in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database so your friend's farm can find it. The record holds the animal's kind, coat, name and traits, your farm's name, a random farm number made by the game, and when the code runs out. It doesn't hold your name, Apple Account, contacts or location. A record that was used stores the name of the farm that welcomed the visitor, so the game can send you a thank-you letter.

Anyone who has the four words could look the record up, so only share codes with people you know. Codes stop working after two days, and a record isn't used for anything once its code has run out. Nothing is sent unless you make or enter a code. We never try to work out who made a record or combine it with any other data. To have a record removed, write to the address below with the code.

Apple runs iCloud under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

The Full farm is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules notes on your iPhone (for example, when a baby animal arrives). They are made on the device and no data is sent anywhere.

Sharing

Postcards and invitations are shared only when you tap Share, through the iPhone's own share sheet, to wherever you choose.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
